Privacy Policy

Social Traction · Last updated August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Social Traction ("the app", "we", "us") collects, how it's used, and how you can request that it be deleted. Social Traction is developed by an individual developer; contact details are at the bottom of this page.

Information we collect

Account information (optional). You can use Social Traction's core features — connecting a platform, viewing your own performance data, AI suggestions, and trends — without creating an account. If you choose to create one anyway (to back up your connected channels and history so you can restore them on a new device), we collect:

  • Your email address
  • Your password (stored securely by our backend provider, Supabase — we never see or store it in plain text)
  • Your full name, if you choose to provide one

Connected social media accounts. If you choose to connect a supported platform (currently YouTube; Instagram support via Meta's official Instagram Login is being added and will use the same authorization flow), you'll be asked to authorize access via that platform's own login or public channel lookup. Once connected, we access and store:

  • Your public profile info on that platform (username/handle, profile picture, subscriber/follower count)
  • Your own posts/videos (titles, media links, thumbnails, publish dates)
  • Performance metrics for your own posts (views, likes, comments, shares, reach)

This data belongs to the account you connect — we only ever request it with your explicit authorization (or, for platforms using public data only, from the channel handle you provide), and only to show it back to you inside Social Traction. Where a platform's connection uses an access token, that token is stored in a separate, locked-down part of our database that the app itself cannot read directly.

Information stored on your device. Social Traction caches your connected accounts' data locally on your device (for offline viewing and performance), and stores your sign-in session in the iOS Keychain, which is encrypted by iOS itself.

How we use your information

We use the information above solely to operate Social Traction: to show you your own connected accounts' performance in the app's dashboard, analysis, suggestions, and trends screens, and, if you create an account, to keep your session signed in across launches. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your data to anyone.

AI-generated suggestions. If you turn on AI Suggestions (off by default — the app asks for your explicit permission before this is ever used, and you can turn it off anytime in Settings), each connected post's title and its view, like, and comment counts are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a suggestion for that post. Only that post's title and those counts are sent — never your email, name, or any other account details. Google processes this data solely to generate the response returned to the app; see Google's Gemini API Additional Terms of Service for how Google itself handles it.

How we share your information

We don't share your personal information with third parties for their own purposes. The only parties involved in processing it are:

  • Supabase, our backend infrastructure provider — hosts our database and handles authentication on our behalf, under its own security and privacy commitments.
  • The platform(s) you connect (e.g. YouTube/Google, or Meta/Instagram once its support is live) — we call their official APIs, using your own authorization or the channel you specify, solely to fetch that account's data.
  • Google's Gemini API — only if you've explicitly turned on AI Suggestions, and only the specific post title and metrics described above, never your account details.

Data security

Every table in our database is protected by row-level security, meaning your data is only ever readable by your own signed-in account. Connected-account access tokens are kept in a separate table that only trusted backend functions can read — never the app itself, and never any other user. All network traffic between the app and our backend is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).

Data retention & deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account exists. You can request full deletion of your account and all associated data at any time — see our Data Deletion Instructions for how.

Children's privacy

Social Traction is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 years old. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us using the details below and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of Social Traction after a change means you accept the updated policy.
