Data Deletion Instructions

Social Traction · Last updated August 9, 2026

You can request permanent deletion of your Social Traction account and all data associated with it at any time. Here's how.

How to request deletion

  1. 1Email hasmicasekar@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account, with the subject "Delete my account."
  2. 2We'll verify the request is coming from the account owner.
  3. 3Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted within 30 days, and we'll confirm by email once it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email, name, password)
  • Any social media accounts you've connected, including their stored access tokens
  • All posts, metrics, and history synced from those accounts
  • Any AI suggestions or trend data associated with your account

Once deletion is processed, this data cannot be recovered.

Disconnecting a single platform instead

If you only want to stop Social Traction accessing one connected platform (e.g. Instagram) rather than delete your whole account, let us know at the email above and we'll disconnect it and remove its stored access token on your behalf.
