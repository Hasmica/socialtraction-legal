Social Traction

Real performance insight for content creators — built by an independent developer.

Social Traction is a mobile app that connects to a creator's own YouTube channel (with more platforms planned) and turns their real performance data into clear, actionable insight — without the manual work of digging through separate dashboards.

What it does

Real performance tracking Reach, views, likes, and comments across a creator's own recent posts, with week-over-week growth.
AI-powered suggestions Specific, data-grounded suggestions comparing each post to the creator's own average — not generic advice.
Real metrics comparison See how each post stacks up against a creator's typical post, computed directly from their own real data.
What's trending Currently trending content on the connected platform, so creators can spot timely opportunities.

About

Social Traction is developed and maintained by an individual developer, Hasmica. The app connects to each creator's own social accounts using that platform's own official login and consent screen, and only ever shows a creator their own data — it is never shared with any other user, sold, or used for advertising. See the Privacy Policy and Data Deletion Instructions for full details.
